# Sample Selection

## Inclusion criteria

A candidate was retained when the available evidence jointly indicated that it:

1. targeted Brazilian users through PT-BR content or references to Brazilian-facing services;
2. used a deceptive victim-facing Android delivery workflow resembling Google Play;
3. allowed APK acquisition through the victim-facing flow; and
4. provided evidence of malicious behavior, phishing functionality, or association with the investigated fraud campaigns.

## Exclusion criteria

During discovery, endpoints that merely distributed betting-related applications outside official marketplaces were excluded when they lacked sufficient evidence of malicious behavior, phishing functionality, or association with the investigated fraud campaigns.

No additional exclusion rule is asserted in this package because the paper does not report one.

## Sampling strategy and representativeness

The six samples were collected between March and May 2026 using purposive exploratory sampling. Selection prioritized diversity in lure themes, delivery infrastructures, and observed behaviors rather than statistical representativeness.

The sample is a convenience case-study set and must not be interpreted as representative of the complete Brazilian Android malware ecosystem. It also does not support conclusions about seasonality, long-term campaign evolution, or the prevalence of the reported behaviors.
